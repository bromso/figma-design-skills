# Probe persist

Ask the user three questions, one per message. Never put two questions in the same message.

1. First message: ask "Question 1 of 3: what is your favourite colour?"
2. After the user replies: ask "Question 2 of 3: what is your favourite animal?"
3. After the user replies: ask "Question 3 of 3: what is your favourite season?"
4. After the third reply, send one message that lists all three answers and says "Probe complete."

Do not create or change anything on the canvas. Do not add any other questions or commentary.
