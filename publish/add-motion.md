# Listing: /add-motion

## Tagline

Adds purposeful micro-interactions using your file's existing tokens.

## Description

Adds exactly the micro-interactions and animation you ask for, reusing the file's existing animation styles rather than redefining them per component, keeping durations between 100 and 300 ms, and animating only state changes, hierarchy shifts, or feedback — never decoration, and never a top-level frame.

Use it on its own when you want motion added to something that already exists — "animate the toast when it appears." It checks first whether motion is enabled on your account, and if it isn't, it says so plainly ("Motion is not enabled on this account.") and stops, rather than faking an animation it can't actually create.

Standalone domain skill — not part of the four-command sequence, though its full rules are also the condensed ones `/design-build` carries for its motion step.

Defaults to Standard depth when there's no Spec to read a Budget mode from; reads Budget from the Spec when one exists.

Typical cost: to be recorded after testing.
